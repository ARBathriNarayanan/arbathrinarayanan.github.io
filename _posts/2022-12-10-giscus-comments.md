Inside a city, I ran for money and fame,

For things I now feel are shallow and lame.

A lone grain buried in the heap of sand,

Chasing my dreams through a sea so grand.

Then came an invite to visit my native place,

Yon small town which raised me with grace.

So I jumped on a wagon, reminiscing times,

When I used to wake up hearing temple chimes.

When I reached, I saw the familiar gopuram*,

The majestic tower the state took its emblem from.

I realise that’s the heirloom I forgot to cherish,

While chasing bright things that will soon perish.

I smile that such a sign was born from mine

That vibrant gopuram still stands over time.
