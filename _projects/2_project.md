The current mirror qubit is a popular concept due to it’s protection from
relaxation and dephasing due to it’s structure. In this project, we study the
properties of the same, examples include the Lagrangian, Hamiltonian and the
equation of motion for the system.

We also form a method to obtain the wave equation for the system, by
transforming into the exciton and agiton subspace, and finding suitable solutions
for the signal to propagate through the qubit.
