There are many different ways quantum computers can be realized, examples including ion traps, Nuclear Magnetic Resonances,
Rydberg qubits, etc. We take the most common approach of using a superconducting chip and first learn about its properties.
We also try to explore various other ways of making and simulating a superconducting chip.
We then do a theoretical study of superconducting qubit system. We also suggest modification to the quoted work by adding a
first order term and fining out the dispersion relation for the complex case. Finally we are trying to model Hawking radiation,
and hence the name ”Analogue Gravity”
