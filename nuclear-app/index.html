Overview

What Does It Do?

Nuclear level schemes map the quantum energy states inside an atomic nucleus. Building them from experimental data currently takes human experts months. This application automates the process — classically and with a quantum solver — making the comparison direct and scientifically meaningful.

📂

Flexible Input

Upload a raw gamma-ray coincidence matrix or a transition list. Both formats produce the same internal directed graph.

🔬

Classical Baseline

BFS reconstruction from the ground state upward, scored with Demand's intensity-weighted energy self-consistency formula.

⚡

QAOA Quantum Solver

Cost Hamiltonian encodes coincidence constraints. PennyLane optimises variational parameters; every 5 steps the Demand Score is tracked.

📊

Real Comparison

Convergence plot shows Demand Score vs. QAOA step — both axes are the same quantity, directly comparable.

💾

4 Export Formats

Level scheme PNG, ENSDF nuclear data file, CSV level table, and full JSON results including energy history.

🔌

IBM Quantum Ready

Switch from Aer simulator to real IBM Quantum hardware with a single API key. Circuit transpilation is automatic.

Demand's Formula

The Scoring Function

Every candidate level scheme is scored with Demand's analytical self-consistency formula. Both solvers minimise the same quantity — making the comparison meaningful.

Demand Score — equation (2) of the proposal
𝒮 = Σᵢ wᵢ · | (Efrom_i − Eto_i) − Eγᵢ |
where   wᵢ = Iᵢ / 1000   (intensity weight)  ·  𝒮 = 0 means perfect self-consistency
Methodology

How It Works

Five steps from raw detector data to a quantum-solved level scheme.

1

Data Ingestion

Upload a coincidence matrix .csv or transition list with columns id, energy_keV, intensity, from_level, to_level. Two test datasets (Ba-133, Co-60) are bundled — no upload needed to start.

2

Graph Construction

Transitions become directed edges in a NetworkX DAG. Ground state is detected algorithmically as the sink node — fully name-agnostic. Works with GS, ground, 0+, or any custom name.

3

Classical Baseline

BFS from ground state upward reconstructs level energies in O(V+E). The Demand Score 𝒮 is computed as the reference. Runs in <1 ms for Ba-133.

4

QAOA Quantum Solver

The cost Hamiltonian encodes intensity rewards and coincidence constraints with chain bonuses (κ = 1.2). PennyLane optimises (γ, β) parameters over N steps with Adam. The bitstring is decoded as a BFS ordering — all transitions always used.

5

Output

Level scheme diagrams (classical in green, quantum in blue), convergence plot with matching Demand Score axes, circuit diagram with optimised parameters, and four export formats.

Technology

Stack

Two distinct quantum primitives, one unified classical-quantum pipeline.

Quantum

PennyLane

QAOA engine with automatic differentiation. Adam optimiser for hybrid classical-quantum loops.

Quantum

Qiskit

Circuit construction, Aer cloud simulator, IBM Quantum hardware access and transpilation.

Quantum (high-level)

qiskit-optimization

Encodes level ordering as a QuadraticProgram (QUBO). Specialised higher-level abstraction — no raw gates needed.

Classical

NetworkX

Directed graph construction and BFS reconstruction of Demand's transition-centric representation.

Coincidence matrix processing, intensity normalisation, statistical scoring.

Development Log

8 Bugs Found & Fixed

A systematic audit of the codebase identified and resolved eight bugs spanning logic, visualisation, and data export.

1
Ground state hardcoded to "GS"

Fixed: detection now uses energy = 0, works for any level naming convention.

2
demand_history uninitialised when quantum solver skipped

Fixed: initialised to [] unconditionally before the quantum branch.

3
demand_history undefined in exception handler

Fixed: explicitly added to the except block so fallback always works.

4
Stale session results after dataset switch

Fixed: session state cleared when dataset name changes.

5
Hamiltonian missing energy self-consistency weighting

Fixed: chain bonus κ = 1.2 added for transitions that feed into each other.

6
Division-by-zero if all intensities are zero

Fixed: max(intensity) or 1 guard added to arrow alpha calculation.

7
CSV export sorted quantum-only levels incorrectly

Fixed: sort key falls back to quantum energy when classical energy is absent.

8
JSON export crashed on NumPy arrays

Fixed: recursive _json_safe() converter applied before json.dumps().
